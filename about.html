about

From healthcare strategy to building agents

I work where healthcare strategy, machine learning and strategic intelligence meet.

At Mayo Clinic I support enterprise strategy work (market assessments, peer benchmarking, operating model design and executive synthesis) and build the AI systems that make that work faster and more consistent.

My focus is turning ambiguous business needs into governed technical solutions: multi-agent workflows, prompt chains, data pipelines and structured outputs with human-in-the-loop review, plus the roadmaps to put them into practice.

Before that I built forecasting models and reporting tools for strategic operations. I hold an M.S. in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics and a graduate certificate in Artificial Intelligence.
