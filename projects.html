  • Wedding seating optimizer

    2025 · Python, NumPy, NetworkX

    Guests become nodes in a weighted relationship graph. Simulated annealing shuffles them between tables, scores each move with a compatibility cost function and gradually “cools” until the seating settles.

    View the repo →
    Problem
    Seating guests across capacity-limited tables so everyone sits with people they know or share a connection with.
    What I built
    Extended an existing simulated-annealing notebook by linanqiu, adding a cost term for whether guests know the bride or the groom, and visualized how the optimization improves.
